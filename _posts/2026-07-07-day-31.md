what_i_learned: |
  Today I focused on strengthening my machine learning pipeline by implementing cross-validation on both the original imbalanced dataset and the balanced dataset. This helped me understand how cross-validation provides a more reliable estimate of model performance by training and testing the model across multiple data splits instead of relying on a single train-test split.
  
  I also explored the differences between evaluating models on imbalanced and balanced data. This gave me a deeper understanding of why balancing techniques, such as SMOTE, can improve a model's ability to identify minority class cases while still requiring evaluation on unseen data. Throughout the process, I relied heavily on visualizations to better interpret the results, making it easier to understand how the models behaved under different training scenarios.
  
  In addition to the model evaluation work, I continued documenting my research by populating my Overleaf paper with the methodology, preprocessing steps, and the progress I have made so far. Organizing my work into a formal research paper helped me connect the coding process with the research methodology behind it.
  
blockers: |
  My biggest challenge today was fully understanding how cross-validation works and how to correctly implement it on both the imbalanced and balanced datasets. Initially, it was difficult to visualize how the data was divided into folds and how the model was evaluated multiple times. My mentor walked me through the process, explained the reasoning behind each step, and helped me interpret the results. Using several visualizations alongside the evaluation metrics made the concept much clearer and gave me more confidence in applying cross-validation correctly.
  
reflection: |
  Today reinforced that building a reliable machine learning model is not only about achieving high accuracy but also about ensuring that the evaluation process is trustworthy. I realized the importance of validating models from multiple perspectives rather than relying on a single result. Every concept I understand more deeply strengthens both my research and my confidence as a data scientist. I am excited to continue into the next phase of model development and compare the performance of the different machine learning approaches under each training strategy.
