what_i_learned: |
  Although I was not in the office today because I was attending a conference, I continued working remotely and made meaningful progress on my research. I finalized the visualizations for my cross-validation results and then moved on to implementing a cost-sensitive learning approach as an alternative to SMOTE for handling the class imbalance in my CHD dataset. This allowed me to explore another strategy for improving model performance without generating synthetic samples. I also documented my methodology, results, and observations in my Overleaf research paper, keeping my manuscript up to date with the latest findings.
  
  Outside of my research, I attended several conference sessions on active listening, dining etiquette, and building a strong LinkedIn profile. Each session offered practical insights that extend beyond technical skills and emphasized the importance of communication, professionalism, and personal branding. I also had the opportunity to network with students from other universities and learn about their research experiences.
  
  One of the highlights of my day was meeting someone who is currently working on a greenhouse project. Since greenhouse farming is an area I hope to pursue in the future, hearing about their work and exchanging ideas was both encouraging and inspiring. It reminded me that meaningful connections often happen when you openly share your interests.
  
blockers: |
  My biggest challenge today was balancing my research responsibilities with being fully present at the conference. Switching between technical work and conference sessions throughout the day left me feeling mentally overstimulated and low on energy. I found it difficult to give my full attention to both without feeling mentally drained.
  
reflection: |
  Today reminded me that growth happens both inside and outside the research lab. While I made steady progress on my machine learning work, I also invested in developing professional skills and expanding my network. Moving forward, I want to be more intentional about time blocking so I can dedicate focused periods to research while still fully engaging in learning opportunities around me. My goal is to manage my energy more effectively so I can remain productive without feeling overwhelmed. I am looking forward to another day of learning, making connections, and continuing to strengthen both my research and professional development.
