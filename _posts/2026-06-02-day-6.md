what_i_learned: |
      Today began with a Figma check-in where participants showcased their mockup assignments and received feedback on their designs. It was interesting to see the different approaches everyone took to solving the same design challenge.
      During our research lab session, we explored data structures and reviewed the Python concepts that will be most relevant to our research project. We spent time working with data through slicing, filtering, and manipulation techniques. These activities helped me better understand how researchers prepare and organize data before applying machine learning models.
      One of the most valuable sessions was Google AI Quests. The activity reinforced the importance of the AI development lifecycle. My key takeaway was that data is the foundation of every AI system. Before building a model, researchers must clearly define the problem, collect relevant data, clean the data, remove missing values and sensitive information, train the model on diverse datasets, and continuously test and improve the system. I appreciated how the quest connected directly to the research process we will be following throughout this project.
      In Python 102, we took a deeper dive into Gradio and learned how to build more advanced AI-powered applications. We explored Groq, generated API keys, and created chatbot interfaces using Gradio. Some of our exercises included building applications that could adjust explanations based on a user's expertise level and creating a fully functional chat application using Gradio Blocks.
  
blockers: |
      I did not encounter any major blockers today. The concepts built naturally on what we had learned in previous sessions, and the hands-on exercises helped reinforce my understanding.

reflection: |
  Today felt like an important bridge between research and application. The literature review session showed me how strong research begins with understanding what has already been studied and identifying meaningful gaps in existing knowledge. At the same time, the Gradio activities demonstrated how quickly AI ideas can be transformed into practical tools and experiences. One theme that stood out throughout the day was the importance of building on existing knowledge. We looked at reviewing research papers, organizing data with nested dictionaries, creating AI applications..., And in all I could see a play of building on foundations. Overall, today was both informative and motivating, and it gave me a clearer picture of how the research process and AI development work together.
