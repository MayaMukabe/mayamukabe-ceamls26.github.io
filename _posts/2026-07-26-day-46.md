what_i_learned: |
  Today marked the final day of the symposium and the conclusion of an unforgettable summer research experience. We began the day by attending the keynote session, which set an inspiring tone before moving into the SAIRI research presentations.
  
  After the keynote, it was finally time to present the work my team and I had dedicated ourselves to throughout the summer. I presented my portion of our research on developing an optimization-driven machine learning framework for predicting respiratory health risks using urban environmental data. It was rewarding to share months of hard work, explain our methodology and findings, and answer questions from the audience. Responding to technical questions challenged me to think critically about our decisions and demonstrated how much I have grown, not only in my technical knowledge but also in my confidence as a presenter.
  
  Later in the day, I participated in the hackathon where my team presented our AI solution designed to promote equitable learning opportunities for K–12 students. It was exciting to see everyone's creativity and the different ways teams applied artificial intelligence to solve real-world problems. I am incredibly proud that our team earned second place. Seeing an idea that started as a brainstorming session grow into an award-winning solution was one of the highlights of my summer.
  
  The symposium concluded with the award ceremony, where we celebrated the accomplishments of the entire cohort. Afterward, I spent time saying goodbye to my mentors, fellow researchers, and new friends. It was truly a bittersweet moment. Over the past several weeks, we had become a community, learning from one another, overcoming challenges together, and celebrating each milestone along the way.
  
blockers: |
  There were no major technical blockers today. The biggest challenge was managing the emotions that came with presenting, competing, and realizing that this incredible journey had come to an end.
  
reflection: |
  Looking back, I am amazed at how much I have grown throughout this internship. I gained hands-on experience in machine learning, data analysis, optimization algorithms, scientific writing, and research presentations. Beyond the technical skills, I learned the value of collaboration, resilience, effective communication, and stepping outside my comfort zone. Presenting our research with confidence and receiving recognition at the hackathon reminded me that growth comes from consistently showing up and embracing every opportunity to learn.
  
  Although the program has ended, I know this is not the end of the journey. It is the beginning of a new chapter. I leave the symposium with new knowledge, meaningful connections, unforgettable memories, and a renewed confidence in my ability to contribute to research that can make a real difference. I am deeply grateful to my mentors, teammates, the SAIRI program, and everyone who invested in my growth this summer. This experience will always be one of the defining moments of my academic journey.
