what_i_learned: |
  Today's literature review session focused on improving our academic writing. We discussed the importance of maintaining a clear writer's voice, using effective transitions between ideas, and ensuring that our writing flows logically from one point to the next.
  In the research lab, we explored data cleaning and preprocessing using the Titanic dataset. We learned how to examine datasets, remove unnecessary variables, handle missing values, create new features, and visualize data using histograms and box plots. We also worked with pandas DataFrames and practiced different types of joins, including inner, left, right, and outer joins. These techniques showed how raw data can be transformed into meaningful information before machine learning models are developed.

reflection: |
  One thing that stood out to me today was how much work happens before building a machine learning model. Data cleaning, preprocessing, and feature engineering are critical steps that can greatly impact the quality of results. I also gained a greater appreciation for clear research writing and how important it is to communicate ideas effectively. Overall, today helped me strengthen both my data analysis skills and my understanding of the research process.
