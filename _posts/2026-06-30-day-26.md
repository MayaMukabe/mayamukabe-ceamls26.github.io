what_i_learned: |
  Today I attended the AWS conference, and it was an incredible experience filled with opportunities to learn, network, and explore emerging technologies. One of the highlights of the day was meeting engineers, entrepreneurs, consultants, and other professionals from different industries. It was inspiring to hear about their career journeys, learn about the work they are doing, and build meaningful connections.
  
  I attended a session titled "Let Your Database Speak: AI Agents for Usage, Capacity, & Cost." In this session, I learned how AI agents powered by Amazon Bedrock and AgentCore can monitor databases, analyze usage patterns, forecast capacity needs, identify cost inefficiencies, and automate operational tasks. It showed me how AI can transform databases from passive storage systems into intelligent tools that actively support decision making and resource management.
  
  I also attended the session "Is AI Going to Take My Cybersecurity Job?" The speaker explained that AI is not replacing cybersecurity professionals but changing the nature of their work. Instead of spending countless hours investigating routine alerts, analysts can use AI to automate repetitive tasks and focus on more advanced responsibilities such as threat hunting, security strategy, and investigating sophisticated attacks. This session reinforced the importance of continuously learning AI alongside cybersecurity skills.
  
blockers: |
  I did not encounter any major blockers today. The conference schedule was packed with so many interesting sessions and exhibits that it was impossible to attend everything. Choosing between concurrent sessions was probably the biggest challenge because there was so much valuable content available.
  
reflection: |
  Today reminded me how quickly technology is evolving and how important it is to stay curious and keep learning. Being surrounded by industry professionals, innovative companies, and cutting edge AI technologies motivated me even more to pursue a career where I can combine artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and data science to solve real world problems. Every conversation I had and every session I attended expanded my perspective on what is possible. I left the conference feeling inspired, encouraged, and excited to continue building my technical skills while growing my professional network. This experience reinforced that learning extends far beyond the classroom and research lab, and I am looking forward to applying the insights I gained to both my internship and future career.
