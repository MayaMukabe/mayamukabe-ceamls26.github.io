what_i_learned: |
  Today's literature review session focused on how Generative AI tools can support the research process. We explored different AI platforms and saw how they can help summarize papers, identify key findings, and highlight important information that researchers might otherwise overlook. After lunch, we had a fun activity where we asked ChatGPT to generate images of us based on what it knows about us, which sparked some interesting and creative results.
  During the research lab sessions, we learned how to analyze and visualize data using frequency tables, cross-tabulations, and descriptive statistics. Using the Titanic dataset, we examined relationships between variables such as survival rates, passenger class, and gender. We also explored different visualization techniques including histograms, box plots, density plots, bar charts, and scatter plots.
  Another important topic was descriptive statistics. We learned about measures of center such as mean and median, measures of spread such as standard deviation and interquartile range, and concepts like skewness and kurtosis that help describe the shape of data distributions. These tools help researchers better understand data before building machine learning models.
