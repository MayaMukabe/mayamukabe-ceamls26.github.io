what_i_learned: |
  Today was our Mid-Summer Symposium, where every research team presented the progress they have made over the past several weeks. It was exciting to see the wide range of projects being conducted across the institute, from healthcare and artificial intelligence to robotics and environmental science. My team presented our work on predicting respiratory health risks in Baltimore using machine learning, and I had the opportunity to share my contributions and findings with faculty, mentors, and fellow researchers.
  
  Listening to the other teams broadened my perspective on how AI and data science can be applied to solve different real-world problems. I also learned different ways of presenting research, communicating technical concepts, and explaining results to an audience with diverse backgrounds.
  
blockers: |
  My biggest challenge today was managing my nerves before presenting. Although I had practiced several times beforehand, I still felt anxious standing in front of an audience. Once I started speaking, I gradually became more comfortable and was able to focus on explaining my work rather than worrying about making mistakes.
  
reflection: |
  Today marked an important milestone in my research journey. Standing in front of an audience and presenting the work my team and I have been developing over the past few weeks reminded me how much I have grown, both technically and personally. I felt proud of how far we have come, and it was rewarding to see everyone's hard work come together in one event. I am especially grateful to my mentors for their guidance, encouragement, and constant support throughout this experience. While today's presentation was a significant achievement, I know there is still more work ahead. I am looking forward to refining our research, improving our machine learning models, and preparing for the final symposium at the end of the program.
