what_i_learned: |
  Today was dedicated to preparing for our upcoming presentations. My team and I reviewed our slide deck carefully, making improvements to ensure everything flowed smoothly and clearly communicated our research. We completed several practice runs where I worked on my timing, pacing, transitions, and overall delivery. The feedback I received from my mentors and teammates helped me identify areas I could improve and gave me a better understanding of what makes a strong research presentation.
  
  Later in the day, our cohort was divided into three groups to present our weekly showcase. This gave me another opportunity to practice presenting in front of an audience before Monday's Mid-Summer Symposium. Although I did not learn any new technical concepts today, I learned that presenting research is a skill that grows through preparation, repetition, and being open to feedback.
  
blockers: |
  My biggest challenge today was overcoming stage fright. I still get nervous when speaking in front of people, and sometimes my confidence drops once I begin presenting. I know my research well, but I want my delivery to reflect that confidence. I believe that with more practice and experience, I will become a stronger and more confident speaker.
  
reflection: |
  Today reminded me that growth is not always about learning a new programming language or building another machine learning model. Sometimes growth comes from becoming more confident in communicating what I already know. Every practice session helped me become a little more comfortable, and every piece of feedback helped me improve. I am truly grateful to my mentors for their patience, encouragement, and willingness to invest in my growth throughout this program. As I look ahead to next week's Mid-Summer Symposium, I am excited to put everything I have learned into practice, continue building my confidence, and represent my team well. I know that every presentation is another step toward becoming not only a better researcher but also a better communicator.
