what_i_learned: |
  Today marked our first in-person day at SAIRI, and it was exciting to finally meet everyone face-to-face. We started by introducing ourselves, sharing our names, majors, where we are from, and our favorite TV shows. It was great getting to know everyone beyond the virtual environment.
  We then reviewed the purpose of the institute, program expectations, milestones, and upcoming deliverables. We learned about the two research symposiums, attendance expectations, daily blog posts, weekly reports, and the end-of-summer deliverables, including a 90-second project pitch, technical paper, research poster, and final presentation. We also reviewed examples of research pitches to better understand how to communicate our projects effectively.
  Later, we attended a Data Analytics Workshop, where we explored how data is collected, analyzed, and used to support decision-making and research. We also participated in an AI Bias and Ethics Workshop that emphasized the importance of fairness, transparency, accountability, and responsible AI development.
  
blockers: |
  I did not face any major blockers today. The only challenge I experienced was keeping up with the presentation slides on the screen for an extended period of time. My glasses do not have blue light filtering, and staring at the screens throughout the presentations gave me a slight headache. Despite that, I was still able to stay engaged and follow along with the sessions.
  
reflection: |
  Today made everything feel more real. Seeing everyone in person, dressed professionally and excited about their research, created a strong sense of community. I left feeling motivated and excited about the work ahead. The sessions on data analytics and AI ethics were especially meaningful because they connected directly to the importance of building AI systems that are not only effective but also fair and trustworthy.
