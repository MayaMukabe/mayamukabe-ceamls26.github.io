what_i_learned: |
  I spent a significant amount of time refining my machine learning work, focusing not just on getting results but on communicating them effectively. One thing that stood out to me is that research is more than building models. It is about telling the story behind the data in a way that others can understand and trust.
  
  I worked on strengthening sections of my research paper, particularly the discussion, conclusion, and future work. Looking back at the results helped me appreciate how much progress has been made throughout this project. Seeing the Douglas Rachford optimized SVM outperform the baseline models reminded me that optimization and persistence often make the difference between a good model and a great one.
  
  Another exciting part of the day was working with my research team on our 90 second project pitch video. The project took weeks of research, experimentation, and results and communicating them in a way that was clear, concise, and engaging. It pushed me to think beyond the technical details and focus on the bigger picture: the problem we are solving, why it matters, and the impact our work can have. It reminded me that being a good researcher also means being an effective communicator.
  
blockers: |
  Today's biggest challenge was balancing technical depth with simplicity.
  
  Whether writing my research paper or explaining machine learning concepts, I found myself constantly asking how to make complex ideas easier to understand without losing their meaning. It is a skill that requires patience because clarity is just as important as correctness.
  
  There was also the challenge of making sure every section of my research flowed together naturally. Writing the results is one thing. Connecting them into a compelling story is another.
  
  reflection: |
  Today's work reminded me that growth happens in the details.
  
  The small improvements matter. Simplifying one explanation. Strengthening one paragraph. Understanding one concept a little better. Making one new connection. Each of these may seem insignificant on its own, but together they build something much greater.
  
  I also realized that learning does not stop once the model performs well. There is another layer of growth in communicating ideas, sharing knowledge, and reflecting on the journey. As I continue developing as a researcher and future AI professional, I want to become someone who not only builds impactful technology but can also explain it clearly and use it responsibly.
  
  Today was another reminder that excellence is built one intentional step at a time.
