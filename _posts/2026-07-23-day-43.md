what_i_learned: |
  Today was dedicated to preparing for our final presentation. My team and I refined our slide deck to make sure the content was clear, well-organized, and easy for the audience to follow. We made small adjustments to improve the flow of the presentation and ensured that each slide communicated the key points without overwhelming the audience.
  
  We also spent a significant amount of time doing practice runs. Each of us presented our assigned sections multiple times, paying close attention to our timing, pacing, and transitions between speakers. As we practiced, we identified areas where our explanations could be simplified and our delivery could be more natural. We also made sure our scripts aligned with the slides' visuals, so we were explaining the content rather than reading directly from the presentation.
  
  One thing I realized today is that presenting research is not just about knowing the technical details. It's also about communicating complex ideas in a way that anyone can understand. That means choosing simple language, highlighting the most important findings, and confidently guiding the audience through the story behind the research.
  
blockers: |
  My biggest challenge today was memorizing my presentation while still sounding natural. I also found it challenging to explain technical concepts concisely without leaving out important information. Finding the right balance between being detailed and being easy to understand took several rounds of practice.
  
reflection: |
  Today's work reminded me that preparation builds confidence. Every practice run helped me become more comfortable with the material and more confident in my delivery. Although I still have some nerves, I can see how much my presentation has improved through repetition and feedback. I'm looking forward to the final presentation, and I'm excited to share the work our team has invested in throughout this research experience. I'm grateful for the guidance from my mentors and teammates, whose feedback has helped me become a stronger communicator as well as a better researcher.
