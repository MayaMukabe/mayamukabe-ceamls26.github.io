what_i_learned: |
  Although the SAIRI program officially concluded yesterday with the symposium, today was a day to slow down, reflect, and prepare for my flight home. After spending the past several weeks immersed in research, presentations, and collaboration, it felt strange knowing that this chapter had come to an end.
  
  One of the biggest things I realized today is how quickly a place can begin to feel like home. In such a short period of time, I grew to love the Morgan State University campus and, more importantly, the people I met throughout the program. My teammates, mentors, and fellow researchers made this experience truly memorable, and I know I will miss working alongside them.
  
  Before everyone departed, I had lunch with one of my teammates, Chanel, to celebrate her birthday. It was a wonderful opportunity to relax, reflect on everything we had accomplished together, and enjoy one last meal before heading back to our different schools. Later in the day, I visited the arcade with my other two teammates and several SAIRI participants. It was the perfect way to end the program, filled with laughter, friendly competition, and memories that I know will stay with me long after this summer.
  
  As I reflected on everything I experienced, I realized just how much I have grown. From conducting machine learning research and writing a research paper to presenting at the symposium, competing in a hackathon, and building meaningful professional relationships, this summer challenged me in the best possible ways. I now feel far more confident heading into recruiting season, equipped not only with stronger technical skills but also with greater confidence in presenting my work, collaborating with teams, and solving real-world problems.
  
blockers: |
  There were no major technical blockers today. The biggest challenge was saying goodbye to the people who made this experience so meaningful. It was bittersweet knowing that our time together had come to an end.
  
reflection: |
  As I leave Baltimore, I carry much more than research experience. I leave with lifelong friendships, incredible mentors, renewed confidence, and a clearer vision of the kind of engineer and researcher I aspire to become. I am deeply grateful to Dr. Naja Mack, Michael, Elijah, and the entire SAIRI team for investing in my growth and creating an environment where I could learn, explore, and challenge myself every day.
  
  This summer has become one of the most meaningful experiences of my academic journey. While the program has ended, I know the lessons, relationships, and opportunities it has opened will continue to shape the next chapter of my career. I am excited for what lies ahead and ready to embrace the opportunities that recruiting season will bring.
