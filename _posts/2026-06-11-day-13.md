what_i_learned: |
  Today was entirely focused on research labs. During the first session, everyone took turns teaching concepts they had learned over the past few weeks. Explaining concepts out loud challenged me to think more deeply about what I know and helped reinforce my understanding. It was interesting to see the different topics my peers chose and the unique ways they explained them.
  In the second session, we were introduced to R, another programming language widely used in data analysis, statistics, and research. I learned about RStudio, R Markdown files, variables, data types, vectors, matrices, functions, loops, and packages. We also explored the Tidyverse, which provides tools for importing, cleaning, manipulating, and analyzing data. One thing I found interesting was how R uses vectors and data frames to organize data and how R Markdown allows researchers to combine code, analysis, and written explanations in one document.
  
blockers: |
  I did not experience any major blockers today. Since R was completely new to me, there were many concepts to absorb, but having prior experience with Python helped me recognize similarities between the two languages.
  
reflection: |
  Today felt like a transition from learning Python concepts to expanding my data analysis toolkit. Teaching others in the morning helped me realize how much I have learned so far, while learning R in the afternoon showed me how much more there is to explore. It was exciting to see another language commonly used in research and data science, and I look forward to comparing it with Python as the program continues.
