what_i_learned: |
  Today was another full day in the research labs, and we focused on learning the fundamentals of LaTeX, a document preparation system commonly used in research and academia.
  We explored how LaTeX can be used to create professional research papers, presentations, and technical documents. Some of the concepts we covered included writing and formatting mathematical equations, creating presentation slides using Beamer, building matrices and advanced mathematical expressions, inserting figures and tables, referencing equations, and creating theorem and proof environments. We also learned how custom commands can be used to simplify repetitive tasks and improve document organization.
  One thing that stood out to me was how powerful LaTeX is for presenting complex information in a clean and professional format. I can already see how useful it will be when we begin writing our technical paper and preparing research deliverables later in the program.
