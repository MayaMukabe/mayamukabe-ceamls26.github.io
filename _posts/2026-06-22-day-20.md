what_i_learned: |
  Today I learned about the timesheet submission process and the importance of accurately documenting my work and hours throughout the research program. In addition, we played a game of Taboo, which encouraged teamwork, communication, and creative thinking while giving us a chance to connect with one another outside of the technical work.
  Technically, learned how to use Overleaf and LaTeX to create professional research documents. I practiced formatting text, creating numbered lists, inserting images with captions, and building tables. I also learned more about the importance of data preparation in research. During our research lab session, we worked with the Environmental_Data_USA dataset and used Python libraries such as Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, and Scikit-learn. I learned how to filter a large dataset to focus on Maryland and Baltimore data, select relevant features for a Chronic Heart Disease (CHD) dataset, and handle missing values by replacing placeholder values and filling them with median values. This helped me better understand the data cleaning process that takes place before analysis and machine learning.
  
blockers: |
  I did not encounter any major blockers today. The only challenge was working with a dataset containing many columns that required careful feature selection, but with guidance and practice, I was able to follow the process successfully.
  
reflection: |
  Today was a productive balance between learning new tools and applying research skills. I enjoyed exploring Overleaf because it showed me how professional research papers and technical reports are created. I also found it rewarding to work directly with our dataset and begin preparing it for future analysis. One thing I am starting to appreciate is that research is not only about building models but also about organizing, cleaning, and understanding data. The work we completed today laid an important foundation for the next stages of our project, and I am excited to continue exploring our dataset and uncovering meaningful insights in the coming days.
