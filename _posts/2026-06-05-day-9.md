what_i_learned: |
  Today's session on Computer Vision introduced me to how computers interpret and analyze visual information. I learned that object detection allows computers to identify and locate multiple objects within an image or video by drawing bounding boxes around them and classifying what they see. The process starts with images being broken down into pixels, which computers analyze to find patterns such as shapes, colors, and textures. Just like humans learn through experience, computers learn to recognize objects by training on large collections of labeled examples.
