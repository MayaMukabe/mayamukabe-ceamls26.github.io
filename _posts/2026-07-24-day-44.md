what_i_learned: |
  Today was focused on making sure we were fully prepared for the upcoming symposium. My team and I completed several practice runs of our presentation to improve our confidence, timing, and delivery. After practicing together, we presented our slides in front of Michael, our program coordinator, who provided valuable feedback on our presentation style, pacing, and clarity. His suggestions helped us identify areas where we could communicate our research more effectively and make our explanations easier for a general audience to understand.
  
  We then participated in the final symposium walkthrough, which gave us a better idea of how the event will flow and allowed us to become familiar with the presentation environment. It was reassuring to see everything come together and helped me feel more prepared for the final presentation.
  
  During the remainder of the day, I shifted my focus back to our research paper. I continued working on the Results and Conclusion sections, making sure our findings were clearly presented and supported by the evaluation metrics. I also contributed to refining the Abstract by summarizing the purpose of the study, the methodology, the key findings, and the overall significance of our work in a concise and meaningful way.
  
  Today's work reminded me that research is not only about producing good results but also about presenting and communicating those results effectively. Whether through a presentation or a research paper, clarity is just as important as the research itself.
  
blockers: |
  One challenge I faced today was balancing preparation for both the symposium presentation and the research paper at the same time. It required constantly switching between practicing my delivery and carefully reviewing technical writing, which made it important to stay organized and focused.
  
reflection: |
  As the symposium draws closer, I can see how much progress we have made since the beginning of the program. Looking back, it's rewarding to see our project evolve from an idea into a complete research study with a polished presentation and paper. I'm grateful for the continuous feedback from our mentors and coordinators, as each round of revisions has strengthened both my communication skills and my understanding of the research. I'm excited to present our work and finish the program on a strong note.
